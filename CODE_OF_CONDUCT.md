# Code of conduct

Lintsure is built in the open, and we want it to be a good place to ask questions, report problems and send changes.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, including with people who are new to the code or to English.
- Talk about the code and the idea, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume the worst.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change, and expect a reason when they do.

## What isn't okay

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual language or attention.
- Comments that put people down for who they are.
- Posting someone's private details without their permission.
- Pushing on after someone has asked you to stop.

## Where this applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space run by the project, and anywhere you speak for it.

## Reporting

Email support@lintsure.com. Reports stay private. We'll reply within a few days, tell you what we decided, and we won't name you without asking.

## What happens next

Depending on what happened, we may ask someone to stop, hide or remove their comments, or block them from the project for a while or for good.
