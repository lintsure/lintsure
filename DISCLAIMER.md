# Disclaimer

**Lintsure is a readiness tool. It is not an audit, a certification, or legal advice.**

- **Code only.** Lintsure checks source code and some configuration files. It cannot see your policies, training, contracts, vendors, infrastructure settings outside the repository, or how your organization actually works. Every framework it covers also requires work outside code.
- **Our reading of public texts.** Rules reflect our own reading of the publicly available text of each framework (for example, the EU AI Act and GDPR as published in the Official Journal of the EU) and of the publicly listed control IDs of SOC 2 and ISO/IEC 27001. Our summaries are written in our own words. They do not reproduce, and are not a substitute for, the official texts or standards.
- **Beta rules.** In version 0.1, all four frameworks and every rule are in Beta: rule mappings to framework controls await review by a qualified compliance professional.
- **Pattern-based checks.** Rules look for patterns within a line or a file. They can miss problems and can flag code that is actually fine.
- **Met is not a pass.** A control marked Met means only that Lintsure's rules found no problem in the files they read. It does not mean the control is satisfied, and it does not mean you are compliant, certified or ready for an audit.
- **People decide.** Lintsure and its agents suggest; they do not decide. Review every finding and every suggested change yourself, and have a qualified person review the results before any audit, certification or business decision.
